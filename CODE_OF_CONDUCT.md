# Code of conduct

Wrangler is a small project about making images smaller. Keep the conversation about that, and
keep it decent.

- Be kind to people, and as blunt as you like about code. "This leaks memory on big TIFFs" is useful. Anything aimed at a person isn't.
- No harassment, bigotry, sexual attention, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.
- Assume people are acting in good faith, and that English may not be their first language.

If someone crosses the line, report it privately through the [Security tab](https://github.com/GenuineMoron/img-wrangler/security) or to the maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who won't stop.

This borrows its spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
