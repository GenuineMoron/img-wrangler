Get Wrangler

Batch image processing for people with a few thousand photos and a deadline.

Wrangler shows every file as it will come out before it writes a thing, and Undo puts the originals back. Free and open source. It all happens on your computer: no accounts, no uploads, no telemetry.

Download for macOS

Or with one line in Terminal

curl -fsSL https://genuinemoron.github.io/img-wrangler/install | sh

Or download a package

Wrangler isn't notarised by Apple yet, so the first time you open a download, macOS refuses. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway. The one line skips that step.

It's an alpha. Undo keeps replaced originals for 30 days, but try it on copies first and say what breaks.

Just the command line

img-wrangler on its own, for scripts and servers. It reads the same recipes as the app. Unpack the archive and put it somewhere on your PATH.

The Arch package installs it alongside the app.

img-wrangler plan ~/Pictures/to-sort -r web-ready
img-wrangler run ~/Pictures/to-sort -r web-ready
img-wrangler undo

Every archive is on the releases page.
