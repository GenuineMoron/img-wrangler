What changed and why

The everyday four come first. Size, format, quality and name are always on screen in the same place. Most jobs are one press per channel: 2048, WebP, Balanced, done.

No knobs. Choices are keys (one press, always visible, no guessing which way to turn). Trade-offs are sliders with printed stops. Any number on a screen can be clicked and typed.

Colour tells you what controls what. Blue is size, green format, yellow quality, pink name; on the main screen the new size is blue, the new weight yellow, the new name pink. Extras have their own colours: trim red, canvas purple.

Product-shot tools stay out of the way. Trim, canvas, sharpen, rotate and metadata sit in the side panel; canvas reuses the Size channel instead of asking for dimensions twice. Steps always run in the same sensible order, so there is nothing to reorder.

Each channel's little screen explains itself. Size draws the old and new shape, format shows browser support, quality shows how big files get at each stop against your size limit, name shows real before and after names.

The meter and Run never move. Bottom right, always: what goes in, what comes out, the saving, where it saves, Undo and Run.
