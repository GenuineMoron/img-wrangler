# Trademark policy

The code is free software under the AGPL. The names "Wrangler" and "img-wrangler" and the
Wrangler logo are trademarks of Sean Hawkey, so people always know what they're getting.

**You may**, without asking:
- say your fork or product is "based on Wrangler" or "compatible with Wrangler"
- use the name to refer to the project in articles, tutorials, talks and reviews
- distribute unmodified builds of official releases under their own name.

**Please ask first** before:
- naming a fork, product or service "Wrangler" or something confusingly similar
- using the logo for anything other than linking to the project.

Forks are welcome. Give yours its own name and logo. Questions: open an issue.
